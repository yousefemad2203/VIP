VIP PERFUME HOUSE — Interactive storefront prototype

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract VIP_Perfume_Site.zip.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts; the site itself is a static HTML/CSS/JS prototype.

WHAT WORKS
- Responsive mobile/desktop layout
- Animated smoky colored background and luxury shelf presentation
- Click a shelf fragrance to open a product detail modal
- Product catalog with gender filters and live search
- Add to bag counter and toast notifications
- Brand grid that searches the catalog

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end prototype. Bottle shapes are CSS-generated visual stand-ins, not licensed product photography or true WebGL bottle models. Prices are demo values in EGP and must be verified before launch. Checkout, accounts, inventory, payment, shipping, and order management are not connected. A production-grade photorealistic 3D build would replace these bottle stand-ins with licensed 3D assets and a Three.js/WebGL scene, and connect a backend/catalog/payment provider.
